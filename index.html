Who I Am

About Me

I'm Phidel Emmanuel Ochieng, a 21-year-old developer from Embu, Kenya, on a mission to create web experiences that make a difference.

My Journey

I started coding in 2023 during my ICT diploma at the University of Embu. What began as curiosity turned into passion when I built my first website for a school project. That moment of seeing my code come to life hooked me forever.

What I Do

I specialize in building responsive, user-friendly websites and web applications. From e-commerce platforms to interactive portfolios, I transform ideas into pixel-perfect reality. My code is clean, my designs are intuitive, and my commitment to quality is unwavering.

Beyond Code

I'm deeply passionate about cybersecurity. Currently pursuing certification through Cisco Networking Academy, I believe in building not just beautiful applications, but secure ones. Every line of code I write considers both user experience and security.

My Approach

I don't just write code—I solve problems. I listen to understand, design to delight, and develop to deliver. Whether it's optimizing load times or crafting the perfect user flow, I sweat the details so you don't have to.
